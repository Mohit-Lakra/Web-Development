Spaghetti Recipie

Ingredients

  • 200g of spaghetti
  • 2 tablespoons of olive oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 400g of canned tomatoes
  • 1 teaspoon of dried oregano
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil leaves for garnish (optional)

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large pan, heat the olive oil over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and sauté for about 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the canned tomatoes to the pan, breaking them up with a spoon. Stir in the dried oregano, salt, and pepper. Let the sauce simmer for about 15-20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. Toss the cooked spaghetti with the tomato sauce until well coated.
  5. Serve hot, garnished with fresh basil leaves if desired. Enjoy your delicious spaghetti!
